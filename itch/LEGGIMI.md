**Halloween** (sabato 31 ottobre), da scaricare e, se va bene, da giocare nel browser. È la stessa demo che andrà su
Steam per il Next Fest. Il gioco intero esce solo su Steam. I testi qui sotto sono **da approvare**.

## La descrizione (in inglese, da incollare)

> **Free demo: the first two nights.**
>
> In 1997, three children vanished at Mama Marina's Splashland. Now you fish the dark bay behind the abandoned water
> park, and you must fill the bucket for the Mother by 6 AM while her children climb aboard. Each one wants something
> different:
>
> - feed Gulpy, or he eats you;
> - keep your eyes on Molly, or she drags the boat under;
> - hide under the tarp while Hatch counts;
> - on the second night, Robin comes for the bucket, and someone in the old park needs watching.
>
> Every second you spend defending yourself is a second you're not fishing.
>
> **The full game** has six nights, an Extra Night, eight monsters and the Mother, 100 undead fish to collect, dives
> into the monsters' dens and more than one ending. Wishlist it on Steam: *(il link, appena c'è la pagina)*
>
> **How to play.** Move the mouse to the edge of the screen (or use the arrow keys) to look around; A and D turn to the
> sides, S turns around. Space or click to cast and strike, hold to reel in. F: throw a fish. C: hide under the tarp. Q
> and E: the light. B: binoculars. Tab: sonar. Full controller support. Play with headphones, in the dark.
>
> **In the browser** it runs best in Chrome or Edge on a computer (it needs WebGL 2). If it's slow, download the
> Windows or Linux version.
>
> Interface and subtitles in 9 languages: English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português (Brasil), 简体中文,
> 日本語, 한국어.
>
> *Contains jump scares and flashing lights. An option reduces flashes and screen shake.*

## L'AI (da approvare)

itch chiede se il progetto è fatto con l'AI generativa (**AI generation disclosure**), e il questionario di Steam
chiede la stessa cosa. La risposta è sì: il codice, i testi del gioco, le descrizioni e le traduzioni sono scritti con
un assistente AI; le scene, i mostri e i suoni sono fatti con Blender e con programmi di suono, anche quelli scritti con
l'assistente. Mentre si gioca l'AI non crea niente. Il testo proposto, per itch e per Steam:

> This game was made with the help of AI tools. Its code, in-game text, descriptions and translations were written
> with an AI assistant; the 3D scenes, monsters and sounds were built with Blender and procedural audio tools, also
> scripted with AI assistance. Nothing is generated by AI while you play.
